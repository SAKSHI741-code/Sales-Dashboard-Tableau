# Sales-and-Customer-Dashboard-Tableau

- Built an interactive Sales and Customer Dashboard to help businesses monitor key sales trends, customer segments, and performance metrics for data-driven decision-making.
- Utilized Tableau for data cleaning and shaping, and designed insightful visualizations to highlight KPIs such as total revenue, customer lifetime value, sales by region, and product performance.
- The dashboard visualizes important metrics including monthly sales trends, top-performing products, customer acquisition rates, and regional sales distribution.
- Developed using Tableau with parameter controls and dynamic filters, it enables users to drill down by time period, region, and customer segment, offering clear and actionable business insights.
